About Brainstorm Infotech

Brainstorm Infotech is a leading provider of Structural and Architectural Steel Detailing services across the United States and Canada. We specialize in producing accurate, construction-ready shop drawings, erection drawings, and 3D BIM models using industry-standard software including Tekla Structures and Advance Steel. Our team of experienced detailers ensures every project meets the highest standards of quality and is delivered on time.

Our Services

STRUCTURAL
Structural Steel Detailing
Shop drawings, erection drawings, anchor bolt plans, and connection details for all types of structural steel buildings.
ARCHITECTURAL
Miscellaneous Steel Detailing
Stairs, railings, handrails, ladders, platforms, catwalks, and all architectural miscellaneous steel components.
BIM
3D BIM Modeling (Tekla)
Full 3D Tekla Structures models with clash detection, material lists, and IFC export for seamless coordination.
CONNECTIONS
Connection Design
Engineering-level connection design including moment frames, shear tabs, end plates, and base plates.
REBAR
Rebar Detailing
Reinforcement bar detailing and bar bending schedules for concrete structures, slabs, and foundations.
SOFTWARE
Advance Steel & AutoCAD
Proficient in Advance Steel, AutoCAD, SDS/2, Revit Structure, and ProSteel for multi-software project delivery.
